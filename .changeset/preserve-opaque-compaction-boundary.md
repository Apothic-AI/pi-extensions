Cancel failed remote compaction when the effective boundary is an opaque Codex checkpoint, including malformed or incompatible checkpoints. Native summarization cannot decode its older context; it must not silently supersede it with only the portability warning. Preserve authorized native fallback before the first opaque boundary or after a legitimate native supersession, and keep fixed, credential-safe diagnostics.
