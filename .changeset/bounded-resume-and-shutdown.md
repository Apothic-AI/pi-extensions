Enforce missing per-invocation safety budgets on resumed agents, including foreground/background calls, nested resumes, mentions, and reused managed threads. Share fresh/resumed turn, token, and tool-call enforcement and terminal outcome mapping; preserve configured grace and tool timeouts. Resume inherits the original invocation's turn budget when not overridden, respects authoritative frontmatter/default policy, validates explicit limits like spawn, and does not let zero erase a configured cap. Nested resumes resolve only their branch-local frontmatter through an in-process budget-policy capability; internal callers without it retain the admitted invocation budget rather than consulting the root registry. Authoritative frontmatter zero remains unlimited after a finite earlier invocation. A retained session's finite or unlimited policy survives later default changes; one manager-resolved cap drives enforcement and queued/running UI counters. Activity updates are invocation-owned and lifetime usage is counted only once.

Dispose grouped notifications and pending batch/nudge timers synchronously at confirmed shutdown, retire invocation-owned foreground progress/spinner callbacks before any teardown await, fence captured delivery callbacks, and preserve managed lifecycle/usage receipts while cooperative child teardown completes. Refused/failed resume admissions retire their own UI callbacks, while admitted lifetimes own final cleanup; mention notifications are fenced after asynchronous admission. Vetoable navigation remains unchanged.

The integrated engine is not imported; managed protocol v4 and payloads are unchanged. Event-based budgets cannot forcibly terminate a provider or tool that ignores abort.
