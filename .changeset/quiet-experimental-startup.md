Keep healthy startup, reload, resume, and new sessions quiet instead of displaying routine experimental warnings. Recall and Analytics retain their experimental lifecycle declarations and documentation without startup notices. Codex Compact shows its short portability warning only when the first remote checkpoint is committed in a UI-capable loaded extension instance; native, failed, and cancelled compactions do not trigger it. Invalid settings, storage failures, fallback failures, and incompatible-checkpoint warnings remain visible. No storage, authorization, checkpoint, or settings schema changes.
