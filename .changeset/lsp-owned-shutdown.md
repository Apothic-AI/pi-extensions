Await cleanup after shutdown, cancellation, and protocol failure, and isolate restart cleanup from the next child. Bound post-KILL close confirmation independently even when signalling returns true, and retain unconfirmed client leases across diagnostics/fix runner invocations for the same actual adapter/launcher/environment and workspace root. Rescan every current owner synchronously after waits before admitting a lease, without blocking concurrent active clients. Canonicalize root and launcher aliases and hash the effective inherited-plus-overridden environment snapshot used for spawning so equivalent calls cannot bypass cleanup guards or retain secrets in lease keys. Release leases only on confirmed close; unrelated adapters, environments, and roots remain available.
